About Us

Know About Tanganyika Herds & Homestead

At Tanganyika Herds & Homestead, we produce fresh, pure, and wholesome organic dairy cow milk straight from our pastures in Kigamboni, Bagamoyo, and Nzega, Tanzania.

Our dairy cows graze freely on nutritious natural grasses, resulting in milk that is rich in nutrients, creamy in texture, and free from synthetic hormones, preservatives, or artificial additives. Whether you need milk for your breakfast table, a bustling restaurant kitchen, or a special catering event, we deliver farm-fresh milk promptly every day.
